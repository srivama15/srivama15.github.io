About

I build infrastructure platforms at LinkedIn that make it safer, faster, and simpler for engineers to provision and operate distributed systems at scale.

Over the past 4 years, I've worked across LinkedIn's core infrastructure stack — from building and operating the platform control plane that powers 50+ infrastructure systems (Espresso, MySQL, Couchbase, Temporal, and others), to designing LinkedIn's greenfield Infrastructure-as-Code platform from the authoring layer through CI/CD and into production.

Earlier, I designed a streaming-based quota auto-rightsizing framework for LinkedIn's Espresso document store and built centralized logging infrastructure for Airflow at LinkedIn scale. Before LinkedIn, I spent 3 years at Goldman Sachs building distributed graph infrastructure for fraud detection and knowledge-graph-powered search systems.
